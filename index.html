Why CARL

How It Works

A verification process that
challenges its own conclusions.

01
Submit
A field professional submits a query -- a regulation reference, a material specification, an inspection checkpoint, a procedural question.
02
Evaluate
CARL evaluates the query against a curated library of governing standards for the deployment domain -- not the open web. Every finding is traced to a source.
03
Verify
The initial finding is challenged by independent verification. Agreements build confidence. Disagreements surface as flags -- never silently passed through.
04
Deliver
The result arrives with a provenance label attached. The professional knows exactly how much weight to put on it -- and what still needs human judgment.
